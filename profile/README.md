<h1 align="center">Nacre</h1>
<p align="center"><strong>NAY-ker</strong> · mother-of-pearl</p>
<p align="center">Mother-of-pearl. Colors shaped by your wallpaper.</p>

- [Nacre](https://github.com/nacre-desktop/nacre): a desktop for Hyprland, with a
  universal launcher, Settings, Colors, Welcome and optional AI and Brain apps.
- [Orient](https://github.com/nacre-desktop/orient): an independent wallpaper-color
  engine, readable dark/light palettes and portable theme templates.

Both projects are in their **0.x development-preview** series. Get started with
[Nacre's user guides](https://github.com/nacre-desktop/nacre#user-guides) or
[try Orient](https://github.com/nacre-desktop/orient#try-it) on its own.
